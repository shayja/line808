The sound

Raw. Deep. Hypnotic.

DJ Line808 is a Techno DJ based in Tel Aviv, shaped by Israel's underground scene. The sets move between raw textures, deep rolling grooves and hypnotic momentum around 130 BPM - dark atmospheres engineered for the dance floor, not the algorithm.

Every set is mixed live: long blends, evolving tension, and a narrative arc that takes a room from warm-up depth to peak-time pressure and back.
