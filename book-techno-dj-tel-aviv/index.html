Bookings

Book a Techno DJ in Tel Aviv

DJ Line808 plays raw, deep and hypnotic techno for club nights, festivals, raves, private events and livestreams - in Tel Aviv, across Israel and abroad. Send a date and a venue, get an answer the same day.

Event types

What Can You Book?

Every set is mixed live and matched to the room - warm-up depth, peak-time pressure, or a full journey from one to the other.

Club Nights

Warm-up, peak-time or closing sets on the venue's CDJ setup. From intimate basement floors to main rooms - the set is built around the timeslot, not a fixed playlist.

Festivals & Outdoor Raves

High-energy festival slots and open-air raves with a hard, hypnotic drive. Big-system techno that keeps a field moving from the first kick to the last.

Private Events & Livestreams

Birthday raves, rooftop parties, brand events, guest mixes and streamed sessions. A full DJ setup can be arranged for venues without gear.

The process

How Booking Works

  1. Send the details. Date, venue or city, type of event and rough timeslot - on WhatsApp or email.
  2. Get an answer the same day. Availability, a quote, and any questions about the room, the crowd and the sound you're after.
  3. Lock it in. Once confirmed, the slot is yours. Set length, style and technical details are agreed up front so the night runs itself.

Not sure what fits your event? Listen to the Forge TLV Sessions first - each mix shows a different side of the sound, from raw deep hypnotic techno to hard peak-time energy.

Coverage

Tel Aviv, All of Israel & Abroad

Home base is Tel Aviv - the heart of Israel's underground techno scene. Bookings are available anywhere in the country: Jerusalem, Haifa, the desert party scene in the south, and everything in between. International slots and festival bookings abroad are welcome too.

For events outside the Tel Aviv area, travel is simply factored into the quote - one message, one price, no surprises.

Good to know

Booking FAQ

How much does it cost to book DJ Line808?

Rates depend on the type of event, set length, location and date. Send the details on WhatsApp or email and you will get a quote the same day - no obligation.

Does DJ Line808 bring equipment?

Both options work. In clubs and festivals the set is played on the venue's CDJ setup. For private events and locations without gear, a full DJ setup can be arranged as part of the booking.

How long is a DJ set?

Flexible - from a tight one-hour club or festival slot to an extended session for a private rave. Set length and style are agreed per event.

Does DJ Line808 play outside Tel Aviv?

Yes. Based in Tel Aviv and available across all of Israel - Jerusalem, Haifa, the south and everywhere in between - as well as for international slots.

What styles of techno can be booked?

The core sound is raw, deep and hypnotic techno around 130 BPM. For peak-time slots the sets push into harder, faster territory. Warm-up sets go deeper and more atmospheric. The set is always matched to the room and the timeslot.

How far in advance should I book?

The earlier the better, especially for weekend dates and festival season. Last-minute bookings are possible when the calendar allows - just ask.
